Longest Substring without repeating charactors -

STEP - I - Understand the Problem Space -

There are some example words like 'aabcabbcca', 'youaregreat' etc
So to solve the substring without repeating the two same char should be avoided and the window shift is required there, so for  the substring we would have to maintain a record of 
previously checked substrings.



STEP - I - How to traverse and logic to substring, substring -

○ Loop through charactors one by one  
    • Loop through the another charactors of the string and print the result.
    • Continue to the first loop if the charactor is matched with another next charactor.
    • Maintain the max result of the substring.

Iterational solution

  result = []
